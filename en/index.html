PREGNO · Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

In short. PREGNO has no accounts and no servers: what you enter (the date, the sex, the names, the birth, the contractions, the hospital bag ticks) stays on your phone and is never passed to ads. The app shows Google AdMob ads: Google collects some device data, according to the choices you make in the consent form.

Who is responsible

PREGNO is developed by MM_devlab (Marco Mastria), the data controller. For any privacy question, write to mastriamarco@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app saves in a private storage area of the phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • the date of the last period (if you enter the due date, the app converts it);
  • the last week you opened and your weekly notification preferences;
  • if you enter it: the babies' sex or whether they are twins;
  • the names on your list, the chosen ones, the Name Cup in progress and its hall of fame;
  • the birth, if you mark it: the day and the names;
  • the times of the contractions recorded with the timer;
  • the ticks in the hospital bag lists.

We don't receive any of this data: the app doesn't send it to any server and never passes it to the ad service.

Android backup. If Android backup is turned on, the system may include this data in your Google account backup, so you find it again on a new phone. It's an Android feature, which you manage from the phone's settings.

Notifications

The start-of-week notification is optional and only turned on if you accept it. It is scheduled on the phone, without going through any server; on the lock screen it only shows "PREGNO". You can turn it off at any time from the app's Settings. After the phone restarts, the app schedules it again: that's why it has Android's permission to receive the boot notice.

Images to share

The images (the week, the name announcement, the birth) are created on the phone. They only leave it if you choose to share them, through Android's share menu and with the app you pick. The announcement and the birth images contain the names you chose: you decide whether to share them, and with whom.

Google AdMob ads

PREGNO is free and shows Google AdMob ads (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Union): at most one per session, when you close the week's card. Ad requests contain nothing of what you enter in the app.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect from the phone:

  • the device's advertising ID and other identifiers;
  • the IP address, from which an approximate location is derived;
  • interactions with the ads;
  • technical and diagnostic data about how the ads work.

On Android, the ads SDK may also use the system's advertising features (Privacy Sandbox), if they are turned on on the phone.

Consent. In the European Union, after the first setup, the app shows Google's consent form. Personalized ads are shown only if you accept them; otherwise Google may show non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data. You can change your mind at any time from the app's Settings, under "Ad privacy options".

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete the advertising ID from Android's settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No analytics or tracking tools other than the ad tools described above.
  • No selling of data: we don't receive any.

Deleting your data

In the app's Settings, "Delete data and stop notifications" erases everything you entered from the phone and stops the notifications. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the phone. For the data collected by Google for ads, the tools and rights described in Google's privacy policy apply.

Your rights

You have the rights provided by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the one in the country where you live (in Italy, where PREGNO is developed: the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it). Since we don't receive the data you enter in the app, you control it directly from your phone; for any question, write to us at the address above.

Age

PREGNO is meant for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Not a medical app

PREGNO gives general information and doesn't replace a doctor or midwife. It doesn't diagnose and doesn't interpret symptoms.

Changes

If the app changes the way it handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
